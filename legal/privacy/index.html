PREVIEW SPECIFICATION / 14 OCTOBER 2026

01

Purpose and current scope

SourcingRecord is a research-stage website and synthetic demo. Pilot and contact forms are explicitly unconnected previews. No form contents are submitted to a server or saved to a lead system.

02

Local preview behavior

Form values remain in the current page memory until reload or clearing. A download is created only when you choose Download summary. The demo uses fictional records and changes only that local scenario.

03

Measurement in this preview

The site maintains a short, in-memory event inspection list containing allowlisted route, offer, source and scenario identifiers. It sends no analytics requests, stores no raw form values, and creates no marketing profile. Campaign strings are sanitized.

04

No raw records or payment

Do not enter supplier-sensitive or transaction-level information. There is no RFQ upload, payment collection, bank connection, ERP write or real supplier contact.

05

Operator details before real collection

The legal controller, recipient inbox, processors, actual retention and deletion contact must be confirmed before any real form intake is enabled. This page is a draft specification, not a completed operational privacy policy.

06

Clear or remove local information

Use Clear form or reload the page to remove form content from page memory. Any summary file you download is on your device and can be deleted there. For questions, use the contact preview; it does not send a message.
