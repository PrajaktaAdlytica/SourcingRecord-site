THE COMMITMENT

Four entry gates.

Agree these before any supervised live RFQ.

01

Repeatable category

Choose a low-risk, repeat purchasing job with comparable source records.

02

Named owner

A sourcing lead owns the scope, source review and purchase decision.

03

Authorized envelope

Document permitted terms and prohibited concessions before a draft.

04

Supplier consent

Participation is voluntary, specific and reversible.

ACCEPTANCE / KILL CRITERIA

Go on reviewed ground truth.

Demo polish, clicks and self-reported AI accuracy do not make a pilot successful.

Proceed

Reviewable positive value

Repeatable category, authorized envelope, supplier consent and positive net value after review effort and relevant fees.

Hold

Ambiguous comparison

Missing permission, incomplete specification, unapproved baseline or unclear authority.

Halt

Relationship or authority harm

Supplier complaints, unauthorized contact, consent bypass, prohibited concessions or manipulated baselines.
