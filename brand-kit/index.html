IDENTITY & POSITIONING

The record is the brand.

Tropic’s confident scale and product-led clarity inform the restraint. SourcingRecord adds its own commercial language: an SR counterform, paper folios, comparison rails and dated annotations.

Primary monogram · negative S through the R

Logo construction

The chosen concept is preserved as traced vector curves. Wordmark masters are outlined artwork, not editable type. Keep proportions fixed; never redraw the S independently.

Clear space: at least ¼ of the mark’s height on every side. Minimum mark 24px; horizontal lockup 180px. Use the dedicated inset favicon at 16–32px.

Use with intent

Pine on ivory or white. Ivory on pine. Black for single-ink applications. No gradients, outlines, rotation, stretch, bevel, gold foil or animated morph of the company mark.

Favicon SVG

Voice & message

Measured, specific, commercially literate. Name the job, source, limitation and next human action. Headline: “Every quote. A clear decision record.” Supporting thesis: “Compare low-risk RFQs and negotiate only inside approved boundaries.”

CTA hierarchy

Primary: Explore demo. Conversion: Discuss a category pilot. Secondary: Review the workflow. Evidence: View source. Supplier: Review participation / Decline participation. Avoid “Start saving now” and “AI beats suppliers.”

02

PALETTE & SEMANTIC COLORS

An ink-and-paper spectrum.

Ivory and white carry the reading. Pine carries action. Ochre marks a reference. Sage, mist, clay and cream distinguish chapters without changing the brand.

Recommended balance

About 60% paper/white, 20% pine, 15% quiet chapter tones and 5% ochre. This is an art-direction starting point, not a rigid per-page quota.

Color meaning

Ochre is decorative, not small text on paper. Use ochre ink for labels. Approved, held, blocked and informational states each have a separate ink and tint. Always include a text label or icon.

Layers & depth

Flat canvas → white record → source annotation. Panels have a 1px divider and 12px radius. Use the 7% pine paper shadow sparingly; reserve the stronger shadow for menus.

Surface transitions

Separate paper and sage with space or a divider. Avoid low-contrast controls over textures. Tables, forms and redlines use solid white islands.

GRIDS, SPACE & ALIGNMENT

A deliberate rhythm.

The ledger uses shared left edges, generous editorial pauses and compact working records. Numbers align right; narrative aligns left.

1248px content / 1408px wide

Tablet ≤960px: 8 columns, 32px margins.
Mobile ≤640px: 4 columns, 16px gaps, 24px margins.

Hero: 5/7 split or a full editorial opening. Article: 704px maximum. Section: 96 / 64 / 48px vertical padding. Card: 24px; working panel: 32px. Navbar: 80px desktop, 72px mobile.

Alignment contract

Headings, body, CTA and captions share a left edge. Center only a short closing statement. Baseline-align paired headings. Prices and totals align right; units are adjacent to quantities. Mobile tables become record cards with visible column labels.

Samples demonstrate visual behavior; they do not send or approve anything.

Button dimensions

Primary/secondary 48px high, 20px horizontal padding, 12px icon gap, radius 6px. Compact product control 40px with ≥44px hit area. Mobile CTA full width only where it aids the flow.

State contract

Default pine → hover #245846 → press #102D24. Continuous 2px focus outline, 3px offset; no delayed focus. Disabled: muted surface + explicit reason. Loading keeps its width and readable “Preparing preview…” label.

Durations & easing

120ms press; 180ms feedback; 240ms hover; 360ms disclosure; 640ms section arrival. Arrival: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1). State: cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1). No long elastic bounce.

Approval actions

No magnetic, flipping or shutter treatment on binding approvals, consent or decline. Stable labels and explicit status. Visual polish cannot imply a completed negotiation or verified saving.

Texture treatment

Paper grain is a soft visual material. Grid, rules, dots and brackets express arrangement. Hatch is for exception areas. Decorative texture opacity ≤8% behind typography; solid surfaces behind fields and data.

Image & video rules

Use these originals as static posters; animate accompanying vector lines. No parallax background drift or fabricated teams. Future video should be an original 6–8 second UI walkthrough, user-initiated with captions and a static poster. No stock negotiation handshake.

Animated maps

Public-domain land silhouette, illustrative nodes, a single route draw. No flags, territory or supplier reach claims. Mobile uses a simplified static footprint plus readable descriptions.

Networks & technology lines

Source → comparable field → named reviewer. Orthogonal connections, 1.5px strokes, blocked branches dashed and labeled. Never random particles or a rotating AI brain.

Process & numbered steps

Chapter numbers and short rails. Human approval is a stop, not a decorative speed bump. Mobile steps stack in chronological order; no sideways scrolling dependency.

Data, figures & institutions

Use countable fictional records, direct labels, units and provenance. Show holds and failed recommendations. Human figures represent roles, not invented staff. Institutional pictograms never imply a certification.

Pilot copy

“Start with one repeatable category. Compare an anonymized closed RFQ, then consider a supervised supplier-consented test.” CTA: Discuss a category pilot.

“Hypothetical test pricing: $2,500 per category pilot; $1,000–$3,500/month per approved category.” No checkout or purchase claim.

Evidence language

Use suggested, held, approved and completed as distinct states. State source, date and assumptions. Do not use customer logos, testimonials, savings counters, “live” feeds or certification badges without real evidence.

Motion & record integrity

Animation cannot change values, imply supplier consent or advance approval. RF-202 remains synthetic. Juniper’s 30-day delivery is held against the 15-day baseline. Stonecap has opted out. Sending, payments and ERP writes remain disabled.

Expansion gate

Expand only when a repeatable category, authorized envelope, supplier consent and reviewed positive net value survive the pilot. Halt on supplier relationship harm or baseline ambiguity. Demo polish is not validation.

RESEARCH & IMPLEMENTATION HANDOFF

A kit you can build from.

Semantic tokens, local fonts, vector masters, original images and standalone motion assets. The preview uses native CSS and SVG; no animation library or external font request is required.

Research conclusions

Original Tropic reference: strong typography, clear product narrative and disciplined action hierarchy. Our interpretation adds tactile source materials and margin annotations. CSS transforms and clip-path cover most interactions. GSAP is an option for complex later timelines, not a dependency in this kit.

Deliverable map

tokens.json + tokens.css · components.css · guide.css · guide.js · logos/ · fonts/ · icons/ · textures/ · heroes/ · images/ · data/ · licenses/. Route plans describe composition and limits, not launched services. Read the IA · Visual research · Logo research.

Tokens JSONTokens CSS

Before production

Connect actual contact and social profile details, implement the consented lead flow, review legal content and data handling, validate every interactive demo state, then test the implemented website on real devices. Nothing in the kit sends supplier messages or collects records.

Accessibility checks

18 solid color pairs verified. Visible focus, persistent labels, semantic form fields, touch behavior and reduced-motion controls are included. Motion pauses offscreen and on page hide. A future website still needs full keyboard, assistive technology and device QA.
