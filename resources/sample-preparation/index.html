PURPOSE / MINIMIZATION

Keep raw records out
of public forms and analytics.

The website captures no sensitive RFQ data. Future Notion tracking should use structured summaries and controlled references, never raw records.

  • Segment, buyer role and present workflow.
  • Permissioned sample count and selected offer.
  • Owner, budget, data-access and reviewer readiness.
  • Reviewed baseline versus outcome, omissions and next step.
