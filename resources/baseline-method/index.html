THE FAILED RECOMMENDATION

The RF-202 calculation
is deliberately held.

The prior purchase is $1,140 at 15 days. Juniper is $1,020 at 30 days. A lower number cannot silently resolve that delivery difference.

ILLUSTRATIVE / SYNTHETIC INPUTS

What the arithmetic actually says.

If comparability were explicitly approved with a zero delivery adjustment, the synthetic arithmetic would be:

Prior basis − quote total$1,140 − $1,020 = $120
Review cost0.5h × $60/h = $30
Scenario before fees$120 − $30 = $90
Whole $2,500 pilot fee on one order$90 − $2,500 = −$2,410

This single-order scenario does not establish positive pilot value. Purchase frequency, allocation and reviewed net value need buyer evidence. Unaccepted offers remain excluded from savings.
