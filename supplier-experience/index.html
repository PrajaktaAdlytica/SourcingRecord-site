SUPPLIER FAQ

Decline has equal standing.

The supplier can stop participation. In the demo, withdrawal blocks further negotiation and invalidates approval.

Does declining affect supplier standing in the concept?

The demo records a participation decision, not a performance or supplier-quality score. Opted-out suppliers cannot be contacted through the preview.

How would complaints be handled in a pilot?

A named sourcing owner must define an agreed human support and escalation route before a live test. Relationship harm is a reason to halt activity and review.

Can the supplier contact a human here?

Use the labeled contact preview to prepare an inquiry. No operational support inbox or response-time promise is fabricated.
