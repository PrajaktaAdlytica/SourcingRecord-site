THE ENVELOPE

The policy envelope is visible.

RF-202 fixes price, quantity, specification, payment and liability terms. Only delivery timing may be proposed inside the 15–30-day range.

PermittedDelivery · 15–30 days
Fixed$1,020 · 200 pairs · SG-A v1
ProhibitedSpecification, payment or liability concessions
AuthorityHuman approval before binding terms

SUPPLIER AUTHORITY

Consent is a guard.
It is not a checkbox to bypass.

Supplier withdrawal invalidates pending approval and stops the draft path. The reviewer can inspect or correct an extraction, but cannot reinstate an opted-out supplier through an override.

REVIEWABILITY

A source for every proposed field.

The record retains source IDs and versions, fixed dates, assumptions, owner and a dated next action. Changes reset the approval reference.

01

Provenance

Keep source page, line, date and version beside the normalized field.

02

Correction

Record a reviewer reason and before/after value when a permitted assumption changes.

03

Handoff

Carry the approval and source references into a draft export with an illustrative watermark.

SYSTEM LIMITS

Concept controls.
No certification claims.

Data-handling responsibilities, system mappings, access roles and legal terms would be assessed before live collection or a supervised pilot. No operating integration or audit certification is implied.

  • No autonomous supplier contact in initial scope.
  • No real sending, binding acceptance or financial actions in the demo.
  • No public raw-RFQ upload or sensitive record collection.
  • No production source-to-pay integration is presented as live.
