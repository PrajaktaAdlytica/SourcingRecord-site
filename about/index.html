OUR APPROACH

Start narrow.
Earn the next category.

The initial scope is one repeatable tail-spend category, anonymized closed RFQs and a supervised supplier-consented live test. No autonomous supplier contact is part of that starting scope.

THE RESEARCH QUESTIONS

What must be proven.

Category-specific normalization and constrained drafts may earn budget only if reviewed net value and supplier experience improve. Incumbent overlap makes differentiation uncertain.

01

A useful comparison

Buyer-adjudicated alternatives and exceptions, with omissions and false positives recorded.

02

A willing participant

Supplier consent and experience that survive the supervised test.

03

A real purchase decision

A paid pilot commitment followed by reviewed positive net value, not a demo completion.
