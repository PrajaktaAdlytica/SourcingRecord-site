FAQ

Questions before a pilot.

The limits are part of the product story.

Is SourcingRecord an operating product?

This website is a research-stage product concept with an isolated synthetic demo. No real customers, live integrations or commercial outcomes are claimed.

What comes before a live test?

A procurement manager supplies permissioned anonymized closed RFQs. The owner reviews whether the comparison identifies missed alternatives before agreeing a supervised live pilot.

Can suppliers decline?

Yes. Participation is voluntary and withdrawal blocks further draft progression. The demo includes Stonecap’s opt-out and a supplier-side transcript.

How would value be measured?

Buyer-verified net value against an approved apples-to-apples baseline, category-manager hours, supplier opt-outs and complaints, and an actual paid-pilot decision. Unaccepted offers are excluded.

Are your forms connected?

No. The pilot and contact forms prepare a local preview summary. Nothing is sent, saved to a lead system or treated as a customer commitment.
